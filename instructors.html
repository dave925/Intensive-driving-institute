Student Protection

Our Zero-Yelling Code of Conduct

At Intensive Driving Institute, we strictly enforce our student-first charter. Learning to drive requires a relaxed nervous system and constructive reinforcement.

If you ever feel uncomfortable with your assigned tutor, our customer care coordinator will immediately reassign you to another instructor with zero awkwardness and zero penalty.

  • Punctual arrival at agreed campus pick-up points
  • Full duration practical time (no cutting lessons short)
  • Continuous dual-pedal emergency readiness
  • Objective progress scorecards after every session
