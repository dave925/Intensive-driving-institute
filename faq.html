For a complete novice, our standard Regular Course takes 1 Month 3 Weeks (Manual GH₵ 1,720 / Auto GH₵ 2,220), which includes structured Highway Code theory and extensive hands-on road practice. For learners needing rapid progress, our Intensive Course is 3 Weeks (Manual GH₵ 2,100 / Auto GH₵ 2,600). For working professionals and students, our Weekend Course is 16 Weeks (Saturdays) (Manual GH₵ 1,720 / Auto GH₵ 2,220 with optional Sunday sessions at GH₵ 50).
Yes, our modern fleet includes both manual transmission (Standard) and air-conditioned automatic vehicles (Comfort). All cars have dual-control pedals. If you qualify on a manual vehicle, your Ghanaian driver's license legally allows you to drive both manual and automatic cars.

If your package includes licensing, we assist you in acquiring your official DVLA learner's permit and biometric registration right after enrollment.

We manage the entire licensing facilitation pipeline: computerized theory test prep with simulated mock exams, biometric registration, eye screening guidance, and official in-traffic road test escort. We also provide the institute vehicle for your official DVLA test day so you drive a car you already feel comfortable in.
Practical lessons are conducted on public roads throughout Greater Accra, centered around our main office located Weija SCC DVLA, Same Building with SSNIT, Accra. We provide training on major test corridors, complex multi-lane roundabouts, and the Mallam-Kasoa Highway, with scheduled pick-ups across Weija, Mallam, McCarthy Hill, Kasoa Barrier, and Greater Accra.
