Common Questions & Direct Answers

For a complete novice, our standard Regular Course takes 6 weeks (18 practical sessions plus theory), which matches DVLA recommended practice hours for muscle memory and confidence. For students with urgent deadlines or traveling soon, our Express Course compresses training into 3 weeks of daily intensive sessions.
Yes, our modern fleet includes both manual transmission (stick-shift) and automatic vehicles. All cars have dual-control pedals. If you qualify on a manual vehicle, your Ghanaian driver's license legally allows you to drive both manual and automatic cars.
No, you do not need to obtain a learner's permit beforehand. As an accredited DVLA driving school, our training vehicles and instructors carry official institutional accreditation. If your package includes licensing, we assist you in acquiring your official DVLA learner's permit and biometric registration right after enrollment.
Yes! We manage the entire licensing facilitation pipeline: computerized theory test prep with simulated mock exams, biometric registration, eye screening guidance, and official in-traffic road test escort. We also provide the institute vehicle for your official DVLA test day so you drive a car you already feel comfortable in.
Practical lessons are conducted on public roads throughout Greater Accra, centered around our training hub at Weija DVLA, SSNIT First Floor, including major testing corridors, roundabouts, residential side-streets, and the Mallam-Kasoa Highway. We offer scheduled pick-ups from Weija DVLA, Mallam Junction, McCarthy Hill, Kasoa Barrier, Dansoman, and other locations across Greater Accra.
Our prices are transparent and range from GH₵ 1,800 for Polishing / Refresher training, GH₵ 2,100 for Student Regular, GH₵ 2,300 for Non-Student Regular, and up to GH₵ 2,950 to GH₵ 3,350 for comprehensive programs with official DVLA driver's license included.
Yes, absolutely! WhatsApp is our most active admissions channel. You can click any of our "Book on WhatsApp" buttons, select your preferred course, transmission, and time slot, and our admissions team will confirm your registration immediately.
Yes! Our Polishing / Refresher course (2 to 4 weeks, 10 hours) is specifically crafted for people who already know basic driving or hold a license, but want to overcome anxiety, practice high-speed motorway driving, or master tight parallel and reverse parking.
You only need to deposit 70% of your course fees to be assigned an instructor, secure your training car, and begin practical road driving immediately. The remaining 30% balance is paid prior to your DVLA test and license processing. We accept Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash), bank transfer, or cash.
We understand that academic deadlines and work commitments arise. As long as you give your instructor or our admissions desk at least 24 hours notice, your missed lesson will be rescheduled without any forfeiture or penalty fee.
To register with the DVLA for a driver's license in Ghana, you will need: (1) A valid Ghana Card (National Identification Card), (2) Minimum age of 18 years, and (3) Passing the official DVLA eye examination conducted at accredited eye care centers. We guide you through each step.
