Our Founding Story

Why Intensive Driving Institute Was Established

Driving in Accra and across Ghana requires more than just knowing how to step on an accelerator. It requires sharp anticipatory instinct, calm defensive reflexes, and rigorous respect for the Ghana Highway Code.

Too many learners in Ghana were subjected to aggressive informal tutors, unroadworthy vehicles, or fraudulent middle-men promising fake licenses without proper road testing.

Intensive Driving Institute was founded to set a new benchmark for excellence. We established structured training syllabi, invested in dual-control modern vehicles, secured full DVLA certification, and pioneered flexible payment solutions so that safe driving education remains accessible to all Ghanaians.

Our Guiding Principles

Mission, Vision & Core Values

The foundation of everything we do at Intensive Driving Institute Ghana.

Our Mission

To produce highly skilled, safety-conscious, and courteous drivers who will reduce road accidents in Ghana through rigorous defensive training, state-of-the-art dual-control vehicles, and supportive coaching.

Our Vision

To be West Africa's benchmark driver education academy, renowned for unmatched student pass rates, ethical operations, cutting-edge training simulators, and holistic road safety advocacy.

Our Philosophy

We believe anyone can become a superb driver given the right instruction, patience, and realistic road scenarios. Mistakes are treated as learning milestones, not reasons for reprimand.
