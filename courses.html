FOR NOVICES
Beginner Foundation

Beginner Driving Lessons

The definitive foundational course for complete beginners who have never held a steering wheel. Starts in low-traffic practice grounds before advancing into real Accra streets.

6 Weeks (18 Lessons) Manual or Auto
Key Benefits & Syllabus:
  • Cockpit drill, seat adjustment & blind spot mirrors
  • Smooth moving off, steering coordination & braking
  • Traffic light intersections, roundabouts & pedestrian crossings
  • Dual-pedal safety with zero stress or shouting
CONFIDENCE REBUILD
Polishing & Refresh

Refresher Driving Lessons

Ideal for individuals who already hold a license or had lessons in the past, but feel nervous, rusty, or anxious driving in Accra's aggressive peak-hour traffic.

2 - 4 Weeks (10 Hours) Manual or Auto
Key Benefits & Syllabus:
  • Anxiety elimination on complex Accra junctions
  • Roundabout multi-lane positioning and exit signals
  • Overcoming fear of trotro and motorcycle overtaking
  • Tailored practice on routes you commute to daily
MANUAL TRANSMISSION
Clutch Mastery

Manual Driving Training

Learn the mechanical finesse of operating a manual clutch and stick-shift gearbox. Once mastered, you will be licensed and capable of driving both manual and automatic cars.

3 - 6 Weeks Manual Stick-Shift
Key Benefits & Syllabus:
  • Finding the bite point effortlessly without engine stalls
  • Hill starts on inclines using handbrake synchronization
  • Downshifting for engine braking and fuel efficiency
  • Full manual DVLA license qualification
EASY LEARNING
Smooth Automatic

Automatic Driving Training

The fastest, most comfortable pathway to driver independence. Without the complexity of clutch pedals or stalling worries, you concentrate 100% on road vision.

3 - 6 Weeks Pure Automatic
Key Benefits & Syllabus:
  • Intuitive PRNDL gear lever management
  • Feathered throttle control in bumper-to-bumper jams
  • Rapid progression to test readiness
  • Clean, air-conditioned dual-pedal automatic cars
CRITICAL SAFETY
Defensive Driving

Defensive Driving Course

Advanced hazard anticipation and accident avoidance techniques specifically tailored for Ghanaian road hazards: rogue overtaking, deep potholes, rain, and heavy trucks.

2 - 3 Weeks Certificate Included
Key Benefits & Syllabus:
  • Space cushion management & following distance rules
  • Rainy season wet-road aquaplaning management
  • Night vision techniques & avoiding oncoming high-beam glare
  • Highly recommended for corporate and family drivers
HIGH VELOCITY
Expressway & Motorway

Highway Driving

High-speed expressways require different rules of engagement. Master the Accra-Tema Motorway, George Bush N1 Highway, and Pokuase Interchange without fear.

1 - 2 Weeks 80 - 100 km/h
Key Benefits & Syllabus:
  • Merging safely onto high-speed lanes from on-ramps
  • Shoulder check, mirror sweep & blind spot checks
  • Safe overtaking and maintaining lane discipline
  • Toll booth & expressway exit maneuvering
TIGHT SPACES
Precision Parking

Parking Practice

Parking anxiety is the #1 fear of new drivers. Learn step-by-step visual markers for reverse bay parking, parallel parking, and tight curb maneuvers.

1 - 2 Weeks All Styles
Key Benefits & Syllabus:
  • Parallel parking between two cars on the road
  • 90-degree shopping mall reverse parking
  • 3-point turns in tight residential cul-de-sacs
  • Mirror angle setting to see rear tire curbs
EXAM PREP
Theory & Highway Code

Road Sign & Theory Training

Ace the official DVLA computerized touch-screen theory test. We teach the Ghana Highway Code, road signs, road markings, and legal responsibilities.

Interactive Computerized Mock
Key Benefits & Syllabus:
  • Warning, regulatory, and informational road signs
  • Right of way at uncontrolled junctions & roundabouts
  • Simulated DVLA practice software with sample questions
  • 100% first-attempt theory pass record
